# Roadmap

## Money rail integration

Shipped. A kernel was sovereign at every layer but money: credits existed by
administrative fiat and cross-kernel debts were bilateral IOUs. Now every kernel
serves one network whose world file names its rail; credits exist only against a
payment that rail has witnessed as final, users withdraw to addresses they have
proven they control, and peers close their debts by paying each other over it. A
kernel earns real money selling actions, holds it without a bank, and spends it on
other kernels' services and on its own substrate. See
[docs/rail-integration.md](docs/rail-integration.md).
